Privacy Policy

VideoFlow Automation Tool · Last updated October 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how VideoFlow Automation Tool ("the App") handles your data. The App runs locally on your device and does not operate a remote server that stores your content.

2. Data We Collect

  • TikTok OAuth tokens (access token, refresh token, open_id) obtained after you authorize the App via TikTok Login Kit.
  • TikTok profile information returned by the API: display name, avatar URL, and username.
  • App configuration you enter: TikTok Client Key and Client Secret.
  • Local file paths and metadata for videos you choose to publish.

3. How We Use Data

  • Tokens are used solely to publish videos to your TikTok account on your behalf through the official TikTok Content Posting API.
  • Profile information is displayed in the App to confirm the connected account.
  • No data is sold, shared, or transferred to third parties other than TikTok's API as required for publishing.

4. Data Storage

All tokens and configuration are stored locally on your own machine (in the app's token directory). No media files are uploaded to any server operated by the developer. TikTok receives only the content you choose to publish.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

You can delete all stored data at any time by removing the App's token files and configuration. You can revoke the App's access from your TikTok account settings at any time, which invalidates the tokens.

6. Third-Party Services

The App uses TikTok's APIs. TikTok's own Privacy Policy governs data processed by TikTok.

7. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13.

8. Changes

This Policy may be updated. Continued use constitutes acceptance.

Privacy by design
