VideoFlow Automation / youtube pipline tool An automation tool that helps creators produce and publish short-form videos to TikTok. It runs on your own machine, renders videos locally, and publishes them to your TikTok account through the official TikTok Content Posting API after you authorize the app. Features: - Connect your TikTok account securely via OAuth 2.0. - Upload rendered videos to your TikTok inbox (draft) or post directly. - Respect TikTok's privacy and content policies. This is a personal automation utility. All media stays on your device; only OAuth tokens are stored locally and can be revoked at any time from the TikTok app.
